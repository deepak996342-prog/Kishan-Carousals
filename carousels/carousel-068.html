The Problem
Why one-size-fits-all email

The wrong message to the wrong person is worse than silence.

Imagine sending a beginner's guide to a seasoned professional, or an enterprise offer to a solopreneur. Wrong message, wrong person.

4 Key Segments
The 4 most valuable ways
to segment your list.
Start with one. Add others as your list grows.

1. By lead magnet — which content they opted in for signals their core interest.
2. By engagement — high openers get different cadence from low openers.
3. By declared preference — ask in onboarding: 'I'm a [solopreneur / agency / corporate]'.
4. By purchase behaviour — clients receive different content from prospects.

Start with one segment. Add others as list grows.

Behaviour-Based
Behaviour-based segmentation —
letting actions define the audience.
Personalised delivery at scale with zero manual effort per subscriber.

Someone who clicks every link about 'LinkedIn strategy' should receive more LinkedIn content. Someone who never clicks 'video content' links needs less of it.

Email platforms track this automatically. Set rules: 3 clicks on [topic] → add to [segment]. Result: personalised content delivery at scale with zero manual effort per subscriber.

Client vs. Prospect
Client vs. prospect segmentation —
the most important divide.
These two groups should never receive the same emails.

Clients need: onboarding content, case study updates, upsell opportunities, recognition.
Prospects need: educational content, social proof, offers, urgency.

A client who keeps getting 'check out my services' emails feels like they didn't matter once they signed.

Segment them from day one.

Segmentation Audit
The segmentation audit — 30 minutes
to a cleaner, more profitable list.
You'll never send to a single unsegmented list again.

Open your email platform. Look at your last 5 emails:
Who opened all 5 (highly engaged), who opened 1–2 (warming), who opened none (cold).

Create 3 segments with these groups. Send each a different message this week. Track conversion rate differences.

You'll never send to a single unsegmented list again.
