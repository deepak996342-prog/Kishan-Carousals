Why Pillars Matter
What content pillars are and
why you need exactly 4.
Without pillars: random content. With pillars: a recognisable brand.

Content pillars are the 3-5 recurring themes your account is known for. Without them: random content, inconsistent audience, algorithm confusion. With them: a recognisable brand, a loyal audience, and an algorithm that knows who to show your content to. 4 pillars is the sweet spot — enough variety to stay interesting, focused enough to build authority.

The 4 Pillars
The 4 pillars for service-business
Instagram accounts.
When in doubt, add value.

Pillar 1: Educational — teaches something, builds authority.
Pillar 2: Social proof — client results, case studies, builds trust.
Pillar 3: Behind-the-scenes — your process, your life, builds connection.
Pillar 4: Promotional — your offer, your services, converts.
Ratio: 50% educational / 25% social proof / 15% BTS / 10% promotional.

Pillars to Formats
Mapping your pillars to
content formats.
Match your message to the format where it performs best.

Educational → carousels and Reels. Social proof → Stories and feed posts. Behind-the-scenes → Stories and Reels. Promotional → feed posts and Lives. Match your message to the format where it performs best. Instagram's algorithm rewards format variety across pillars. Using all features keeps your content visible to the widest audience.

30 Days of Content
Creating 30 days of content
from 4 pillars.
Batch-create by pillar to eliminate daily content decisions.

If you post once per day: 15 educational, 7 social proof, 5 BTS, 3 promotional. Batch-create by pillar: write all 15 educational posts in one session. All 7 social proof posts in another. This approach eliminates daily content decisions and creates consistency. Batch-created content is consistently better than content made under daily pressure.

Content Audit
Auditing your existing content
with the pillar lens.
Most unoptimised accounts: 60%+ promotional.

Look at your last 30 posts. Categorise each by pillar. Most unoptimised accounts: 60%+ promotional, very little educational. This is why followers don't engage — they feel marketed to, not served. Rebalance toward educational and social proof. Watch engagement improve within 30 days. Data doesn't lie — let your audit guide the shift.
