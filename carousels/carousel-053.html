The Intimacy Advantage
Why Stories convert better
than feed posts.
Stories are the most intimate format on any platform.

Stories are intimate. No likes, no public comments — just you and your viewer. The reply button on a Story is the lowest-friction DM trigger on any platform. 500 million people watch Stories daily. Your warmest audience sees your Stories first — before they ever check the feed. This is your most valuable real estate.

7-Day Cycle
The 7-day Stories content cycle.
Consistent without requiring daily creativity.

Monday: share a client win. Tuesday: quick tip relevant to your audience. Wednesday: poll ('Which is harder: X or Y?'). Thursday: behind-the-scenes of your work. Friday: personal story that connects to your values. Saturday: reshare a piece of content. Sunday: preview of the coming week. This cycle keeps you consistently visible without requiring daily creativity.

Interactive Features
Stories features that generate
conversation.
The algorithm shows Stories to people who engage with them.

Polls get replied to. Questions generate DMs. Countdown stickers create anticipation. Quizzes build engagement and educate. The algorithm shows your Stories to people who regularly engage with them. Features that invite interaction = more reach to your warmest audience. Use interactive elements every single day.

Highlights Strategy
Pinning highlights — your
permanent portfolio.
Highlights survive past 24 hours and become part of your profile.

Categories for service businesses: Client Results / My Process / About Me / FAQ / Free Resources. Each Highlight is a mini landing page. A prospect can learn everything they need to know without scrolling your feed. Well-organised Highlights show that you take your business seriously. They're the first thing a warm lead explores.

Story-to-DM Pipeline
The story-to-DM pipeline.
80% pure value, 20% soft CTAs.

Share a client result → add 'DM me if you want this for your business' as text. The ratio: 80% pure value / 20% soft CTAs. Never direct-pitch in Stories. The best CTA is a genuine question: 'Have you tried this? Hit reply.' Conversations start with curiosity, not pitches. The DM from a Story opener is the warmest conversation you can have.
