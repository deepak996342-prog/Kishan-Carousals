New Audience
Why Shorts and Reels are the fastest
path to a new audience.
Short-form reaches non-subscribers. Long-form converts subscribers.

The YouTube algorithm pushes Shorts to viewers who have never seen your channel. This is the fastest form of organic discovery available to creators in 2024. Use Shorts to attract, long-form to convert.
The relationship: Shorts = traffic. Long-form = trust. Email = conversion. Each format serves a different job in the funnel. Most creators treat all formats as equal. They're not. Shorts bring strangers. Long-form turns strangers into followers. Email turns followers into clients.

4 Short Types
The 4 types of Shorts that
consistently perform.
Each type serves a different stage of the audience journey.

1. Quick tips (under 60s): one actionable insight, immediately applicable. Attracts people actively looking for solutions.
2. Behind the scenes: shows the human behind the brand. Builds relatability and trust.
3. Before/after results: social proof in under 60 seconds. The fastest trust-builder available.
4. Hot takes on industry trends: controversial enough to spark engagement, specific enough to attract your niche.
Rotate between all four. Each serves a different audience stage.

10x Content System
Repurposing long-form into Shorts —
the 10x content system.
One long-form video equals 3–5 Shorts. No extra ideas needed.

One 10-minute video = 3–5 Shorts. Extract the most quotable, insightful, or surprising moments. Add captions to every Short — 85% of viewers watch without sound.
Reframe the hook slightly for a cold audience who hasn't seen the full video. The original context doesn't exist for a Short viewer. Each Short must stand alone and still be compelling. The result: one hour of production effort becomes an entire week of content across YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok simultaneously.

Posting Frequency
The posting frequency debate —
how many Shorts is enough.
Consistency beats volume. The algorithm rewards reliability.

3 Shorts per week published consistently outperforms 10 per week for 2 weeks then nothing. The YouTube algorithm rewards channels it can rely on. An inconsistent posting pattern signals an unreliable channel — and YouTube stops pushing unreliable channels.
The minimum viable commitment: 3 Shorts per week for 90 days. That's 36 Shorts. If 3 perform above average, repurpose those styles. If you can only commit to 2 per week, do 2 — but never miss a week. Consistency is the only strategy that always works.

Converting Viewers
Converting Short viewers into
subscribers and clients.
60 seconds of attention requires a clear bridge to longer content.

End every Short with a teaser of the full video: "I break down all 5 steps in the full video — link in bio." Use pinned comments to direct to full content. Include a pattern like "follow for the full breakdown" inside the video itself.
Short-form attention is 60 seconds. Conversion requires longer. Your job isn't to convert inside the Short — it's to make the viewer want more. The Short is the preview. The long-form video is the film. The email list is the relationship. Each step earns the next.
