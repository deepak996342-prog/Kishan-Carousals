Retainer Structure
What to include in a retainer —
the recurring value principle.
Retainers work when scope is clear and value is ongoing.

Best retainer structures for content professionals: Ongoing content strategy (monthly planning + creation + distribution). Advisory/fractional CMO (monthly strategy calls + guidance). Performance optimisation (monthly review + content updates + A/B testing).

The key: recurring deliverables that deliver recurring value. If the client can't point to what they received each month, the retainer won't survive month 4.

Pricing
Pricing your retainer —
the monthly value approach.
Price by monthly value delivered, not hours spent.

Don't price by hours. Price by monthly value delivered.

Ask: what's the minimum monthly outcome this client needs to justify the investment? If one new client from your work is worth £5,000: a £2,000 retainer = 2.5x monthly ROI minimum. A £3,500 retainer = 1.4x ROI.

Let the client's value metric set your floor, not your hours. Hours are an input. Value is an outcome. Clients pay for outcomes.

The pricing formula: Minimum monthly client value ÷ 3 = your floor retainer price. Price above your floor. Never below it.

The Transition
The retainer conversation —
transitioning project clients.
The best time: immediately after strong project results.

'I noticed [result you just delivered]. I offer an ongoing arrangement where we [description of retainer]. Clients in similar situations typically see [6-month outcome]. Would you like me to put together what that would look like for your business?'

Frame as a natural extension, not a new pitch. The project proved capability. The retainer locks in continued results. You're offering them the guarantee, not just the service.

Retention
Retainer retention —
keeping clients for 12+ months.
The enemy of retainer longevity: invisible value.

If clients can't see what they're getting month by month, they cancel. Monthly reporting is non-negotiable: 'Here's what we did, what it achieved, what we're doing next month.'

Monthly 30-minute strategy calls. Unexpected value: surprise them with something they didn't ask for. Visible value creates long-term retainers.

The clients who cancel aren't unhappy — they're just unsure it's still working. Make it impossible to be unsure.

The retention formula: Monthly report + strategy call + one unexpected value item. Clients who receive all three renew at 85%+ rates.
