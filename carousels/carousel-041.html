Why YouTube
Why YouTube is the highest-ROI
content platform for service businesses.
The content you create today keeps bringing leads 3 years from now.

YouTube has 2.7B users. Unlike LinkedIn posts that disappear, YouTube videos rank for years. YouTube compounds where other platforms decay. A LinkedIn post is dead in 48 hours. A YouTube video can generate leads every single month for years.
The key insight: when someone searches "how to [your service]" on Google, YouTube videos appear above websites. You don't need a big audience — you need the right video in front of the right person at the right moment.

Start with 3 search-intent videos: 'How to [problem you solve]' · 'Best [service you offer]' · '[your niche] mistakes to avoid'

First 10 Videos
Your first 10 YouTube videos —
what to create and in what order.
The order matters more than the topics.

Most people start with their best ideas and burn out. Instead: videos 1–3 should answer the most common questions your clients ask. Videos 4–6 should show your process and methodology. Videos 7–10 should be case studies and results.
This sequence builds trust systematically. By video 10, a viewer knows exactly what you do, how you do it, and what results they can expect.

YouTube SEO
The YouTube SEO formula that
gets your videos found.
Your title is a search query, not a creative headline.

YouTube is a search engine. 70% of watch time comes from search and recommended videos. Your title must contain the exact phrase people search. Use YouTube's autocomplete to find real search terms. The description should include your keyword in the first 2 sentences.
Tags matter less than the title and thumbnail combined. The retention rate in the first 30 seconds determines everything else.

No Camera Needed
Creating YouTube content
without showing your face.
The camera-shy consultant's complete guide.

Screen recordings. Slides with voiceover. Animated explainers. Interview formats where you're off-camera. If you're not ready to be on camera, none of these formats require it. Start with screen recordings of your process — these often outperform talking-head videos because they provide immediate practical value.
The permission slip: you don't have to show your face to build authority.

Converting Viewers
Converting YouTube viewers
into discovery calls.
Views without conversion is a vanity hobby, not a business.

Every video needs a CTA at the 70% mark — not the end, not the beginning. Link to a free resource in the description (not your website — a specific landing page). Use end cards to direct viewers to your 3 best performing videos. Pin a comment with your CTA.
Mention your offer naturally within the value delivery. YouTube is top-of-funnel; your email list and DMs convert.
