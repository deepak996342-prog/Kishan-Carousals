Trust Ladder
The trust ladder — how YouTube
viewers become clients.
Each step reduces friction. Skip steps and conversion fails.

The full journey: Stranger → Viewer → Subscriber → Email Subscriber → Discovery Call → Client.
Each step reduces friction and builds trust. Most service businesses try to jump from Viewer directly to Client — putting their offer in the first 10 seconds of video 1. Viewers don't convert at that stage. They don't know you yet.
The patience required: let the trust ladder do its job. Viewers who complete the full journey convert at 5–10x the rate of cold outreach. YouTube is a trust machine. Let it run.

Channel Optimisation
Optimising your channel page
for client conversion.
Your channel page is a landing page. Treat it like one.

Most creators treat the channel page as an afterthought. It's the first thing a potential client sees when they click your name. Channel description: state what you do, who you help, and what they get — in the first two sentences. Featured section: link your best lead-generating video and your free resource. About section: include a CTA and a direct contact method.
A well-optimised channel page converts at 3–5x the rate of an unoptimised one. 30 minutes of setup work. Permanent conversion improvement.

CTA Formula
The video CTA formula that
books discovery calls.
The softer the language, the higher the conversion rate for service businesses.

The formula at 70% of every video: "[Client name] came to me with [specific problem]. After [timeframe], they achieved [specific result]. If you want the same for your business, I offer a free 30-minute audit. Link is in the description."
No hard sell. No price mention. No features list. A genuine story followed by a genuine offer. The softer the language, the higher the conversion rate for service businesses. Hard CTAs trigger resistance. Soft CTAs trigger curiosity. Curiosity books calls. Resistance doesn't.

Community Posts
Community posts and YouTube email —
the overlooked conversion tools.
Treat YouTube community posts like a newsletter. They reach subscribers directly.

YouTube's community post feature reaches subscribers in their feed — outside of videos. Weekly tips, polls, behind-the-scenes moments, and upcoming video previews all build familiarity between uploads.
YouTube doesn't have DMs — but community posts build the familiarity that drives subscribers to find your contact details and reach out. The pattern: video → community post → email list → DM or email enquiry. Each touchpoint warms the relationship. By the time a viewer contacts you, they already feel like they know you — because through 6 months of video and community posts, they kind of do.

90-Day Case Study
The 90-day YouTube launch that
generated my first YouTube client.
Case study videos consistently drive more enquiries than any other type.

Day 1–30: 4 SEO-optimised search videos on the most common problems my ideal clients face.
Day 31–60: first client case study video — specific results, specific client (with permission), specific timeframe.
Day 61–90: process video with embedded offer at the 70% mark.
The case study video consistently drives more enquiries than any other format. Why: potential clients recognise themselves in the story. They see a problem they have, a result they want, and a path from one to the other. Share real results. Be specific. Clients recognise themselves in specificity.
