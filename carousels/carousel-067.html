The Problem
What cold subscribers cost
your deliverability.
A disengaged list hurts everyone on it.

Email deliverability is determined by engagement rates. If 60% of your list never opens your emails, Gmail starts routing ALL your emails — including to engaged subscribers — into the spam folder.

A list of 5,000 with 20% engagement is less valuable than a list of 2,000 with 50% engagement. Your highest-value subscribers are being affected by your inactive ones.

Industry benchmark: if your open rate drops below 15%, your deliverability is at risk. Check it monthly.

Email 1
The personal re-engagement opener.
Week 1: genuine curiosity, zero pitch.

Subject: 'Have we lost you?'
Opening: 'I noticed you haven't been opening my emails lately. That's okay — maybe my content hasn't been relevant to where you are right now. I'd genuinely love to know: what would make this worth opening each week? Reply with one word — I read every response.'

This email gets a 15–25% reply rate from cold subscribers. The reply itself re-engages them with the algorithm.

Email 2
The 'here's what you missed'
value bomb.
Week 2: your best content, curated and delivered.

Subject: 'The best of what you missed (3 things).'
Content: 'While I haven't heard from you, I've shared some things that generated a lot of replies. Here are the 3 most useful: [link 1 — highest engagement]. [link 2]. [link 3].'

The goal: remind them why they subscribed. Show the quality they've been missing. No pitch. Pure value. This email reactivates 10–15% of cold subscribers.

Email 3
The graceful goodbye that
often gets a reply.
Week 3: remove pressure and watch what happens.

Subject: 'Last email from me unless you want to stay.'
Body: 'I'm going to stop emailing you after this — I don't want to be a nuisance. But if you'd like to stay subscribed, click the button below (no need to do anything else). If the timing isn't right, I understand. The door is always open.'

Include a large 'Keep me subscribed' button. Expected outcome: 20–30% re-subscribe actively. Remove the rest without guilt.

After the Sequence
Removing unresponsive subscribers —
the counterintuitive list growth strategy.
A smaller, cleaner list is worth more.

After the 3-email sequence, delete everyone who didn't re-engage. Yes, delete. Not archive — delete.

Your deliverability improves immediately. Your open rate jumps (sometimes by 20+ percentage points). Your metrics become accurate again. The subscribers who remain are genuinely interested.

Ironically, removing 40% of your list often increases your revenue — because you're now reaching only people who want to hear from you.
