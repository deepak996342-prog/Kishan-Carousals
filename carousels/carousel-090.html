Why You Need a Pipeline
Why you need a pipeline —
even if it's just a spreadsheet.
Visibility into every open opportunity — what it needs to move forward.

Without a pipeline: leads fall through the cracks. You forget to follow up. You can't predict next month's revenue. You can't see which stage is losing deals.

Even a simple spreadsheet — 5 columns, each lead in one row — gives you visibility into every open opportunity and exactly what it needs to move forward.

You don't need expensive CRM software to start. You need five columns and the discipline to update them.

Minimum viable pipeline: Name · Source · Stage (1-5) · Value · Next action · Date. Six columns. Everything you need.

The 5-Stage Pipeline
The 5-stage service business
pipeline.
Every opportunity lives in exactly one stage with a specific next action.

Stage 1 Awareness: Lead engaged with content or was referred. Stage 2 Interest: Lead reached out or requested a call. Stage 3 Discovery: Call completed, proposal pending. Stage 4 Proposal: Proposal sent, awaiting decision. Stage 5 Closed: Won (client) or Lost (with reason).

Every opportunity lives in exactly one stage. Each has a specific next action. Ambiguity in your pipeline = lost revenue.

Pipeline Metrics
The pipeline metrics that
predict your revenue.
Three numbers tell you exactly where your pipeline leaks.

Conversion rate per stage (what % move from stage 3 to 4? 4 to 5?). Average deal size per stage. Time in each stage (how long do leads sit at 'proposal sent'?).

These three numbers tell you exactly where your sales process leaks and where to focus improvement. Most service businesses lose most deals at Stage 4.

If you measure nothing, you can improve nothing. Start here.

The Stage 4 fix: If deals stall after proposal, your follow-up sequence or your proposal walkthrough habit needs attention — not your price.

Weekly Review
The weekly 20-minute
pipeline review.
Leads not actively moving are actively cooling.

Every Monday: review all Stage 3 and Stage 4 opportunities. For each: has the next action been completed? Is a follow-up due? Is the lead stale (2+ weeks with no movement)?

Take action on every open deal within 24 hours.

Leads that aren't actively moving are actively cooling. The pipeline review is the most revenue-protective habit in any service business. 20 minutes. Every Monday. No exceptions.
