3 Types of SEO
The 3 types every service
business needs to use.
Each type serves a different stage of the buyer's journey.

1. Local SEO: Google Maps, '[service] in [city]' — for clients searching nearby.
2. Service SEO: 'How to [problem you solve]', 'best [service you offer]' — for clients searching solutions.
3. Thought leadership SEO: questions your ideal client types into Google late at night — for clients in early discovery.
Each type targets a different mindset. Combined, they create a comprehensive organic presence that captures buyers at every stage — from first realisation to ready-to-hire.

Free Keyword Research
The method that costs
nothing but 10 minutes.
Google tells you exactly what people search — for free.

Go to Google. Type your service + a space. Note the autocomplete suggestions — these are real searches from real people. Repeat with: 'how to [your service]', '[your service] for [your client type]', '[your service] mistakes'.
You now have 20–30 real keyword opportunities. No tools needed to start. This is Google handing you your content strategy for free. The autocomplete data reflects millions of actual searches — there is no more reliable source of keyword truth.

Pro tip: Do this in incognito mode to remove personalisation bias. Screenshot every autocomplete result. You now have a 3-month content calendar.

On-Page SEO
The 6 things that cover
80% of on-page SEO.
Master these before anything else.

1. Title tag: include keyword in first 60 characters.
2. Meta description: clear benefit + keyword.
3. H1: one per page, contains your target keyword.
4. First paragraph: introduce keyword naturally within 100 words.
5. Internal links: connect every page to at least 2 related pages.
6. Image alt text: descriptive text with keyword context.
These 6 elements cover 80% of on-page SEO. Master them before worrying about anything more advanced.
