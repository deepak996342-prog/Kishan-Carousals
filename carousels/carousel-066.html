Perfect Welcome Email
The anatomy of a
perfect welcome email.
The reply request is the most important element.

Subject: 'Here's what you asked for + something extra.'
Opening: deliver the lead magnet in the first line.
Body: who you are (2–3 sentences). What they can expect (content type + frequency).
One question: 'What's your biggest challenge with [topic]?'
CTA: reply button.

The reply request trains the inbox algorithm that you're not spam.

Setting Expectations
Setting expectations — the
professional courtesy that reduces unsubscribes.
This single paragraph reduces unsubscribes by 30%.

'Every Tuesday, you'll receive [exactly what]. It takes under 5 minutes to read. You can reply any time — I read every response.'

People stay when they know what to expect. Surprises in email feel like violations of trust. Set the expectation and deliver on it — every single week.

A/B Testing
A/B testing your
welcome email.
Your welcome email is the highest-leverage page in your business.

Test the subject line first (30 days). Then test the CTA (reply vs. click a link). Then test the length (short vs. long). Make one change per test.

A 10% improvement in your welcome email compresses across every future campaign. A subscriber who's impressed on day one opens 40% more future emails than one who wasn't.

Welcome Email Template Structure

Each section and its purpose

Subject Line
'Here's what you asked for + something extra' — deliver promise + add unexpected value
Opening Line
Lead magnet delivery immediately — fulfil the promise before anything else
Who You Are
2–3 sentences on your why — not your CV, your human reason for doing this work
What to Expect
Content type + frequency + 'I read every reply' — sets expectation, reduces unsubscribes
The Question
'What's your biggest challenge with [topic]?' — trains inbox, generates insight, builds relationship
CTA
Reply button only — one action, makes it feel personal not promotional
Sign-off
First name + your role — warm, not corporate, reinforces the human connection
The welcome email sets the tone for everything that follows. Get it right and every subsequent email benefits from the trust you built here.
