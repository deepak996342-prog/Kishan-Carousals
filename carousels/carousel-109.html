Why It Matters
Why the Featured Section
Is Your Most Valuable Profile Real Estate
It's the first thing most visitors see after your headline.

When someone visits your profile, they read your headline, glance at your photo, then scroll — and the first substantial content they hit is your Featured Section. This is your conversion window.

Most LinkedIn profiles leave this section completely empty. Or they fill it with random posts from two years ago. Or they add a link to their company website with zero context.

Profile visitors are making a decision about you in 8–12 seconds. Your Featured Section can either accelerate that decision toward "I want to reach out" — or miss the opportunity entirely. Most profiles miss it entirely.

Item 1
Lead Magnet or Free Resource —
Your Highest-ROI Featured Item
Turns passive profile views into warm email subscribers automatically.

Your Featured Section can work as a silent list-building engine even when you're asleep. A well-positioned free resource — a checklist, a template, a short guide, a mini-course — gives profile visitors a reason to take action beyond just "connecting."

Your LinkedIn audience and your email list are different assets. Your LinkedIn followers can be algorithmically suppressed tomorrow. Your email list is yours permanently.

Put your best free resource in slot 1 of Featured. Give it a compelling title that communicates the outcome, not just the format. "10 LinkedIn Post Templates" is weaker than "The LinkedIn Post Templates That Generated $40k in 6 Months."

Item 2
Your Best-Performing Post —
Show Your Thinking Before Any Conversation
Pre-warm profile visitors with your highest-value content.

Someone who finds your profile hasn't read any of your content. They only know what your headline says. Your second Featured item is the chance to immediately demonstrate what you know — before they've spent any time deciding whether to reach out.

Pin your single best post. Not your most recent. Not your most liked. Your most valuable — the one that best represents your thinking and would make a cold visitor say "this person really understands this space."

A strong post in your Featured section does the pre-selling for you. When someone eventually DMs you, they've already read your best content. The conversation starts warmer than any cold approach could.

Item 3
Client Case Study or Testimonial —
Specific Converts Better Than Vague
Proof beats promise every single time.

A strong case study in your Featured section does more conversion work than any sales page you could write about yourself. The formula is always the same:

Specific problem → specific solution → specific number.

"We helped a B2B SaaS founder go from 0 leads to 8 qualified calls per month through LinkedIn content in 90 days" will convert visitors into inquiries. "We help clients achieve results" will not.

If you don't have a written case study, a screenshot of a strong recommendation or a direct message from a happy client works just as well. The specificity is what matters, not the format.

Items 4 & 5
Service Page & Newsletter —
Only Show If They Convert
High-intent items for the 15% of visitors who are ready to act now.

Item 4 — Service or booking page. Only include this if your landing page actually converts. A poorly designed services page listed in your Featured section can actively damage your credibility. If it looks like it was built in 2012, don't link to it. Fix the page first, then feature it.

Item 5 — Newsletter sign-up. Secondary email capture for visitors who didn't take the free resource in slot 1. Works best when the newsletter has a clear value proposition in its title ("Weekly LinkedIn growth tactics for B2B founders" beats "My Newsletter").

Review and test all 5 items monthly. The combination that works in month 1 may not be optimal in month 4.

Featured Section Audit

What to Remove / What to Add / Testing Cadence

Remove These Immediately
Generic company website link with no context
Random posts from 2+ years ago
Low-performing posts (under 200 impressions)
Content unrelated to your current niche
Links to broken or outdated pages
More than 5 items total (dilutes attention)
Add These in Order
Free resource with outcome-focused title
Your single best post (most valuable, not most liked)
Case study with specific problem + result + number
Service/booking page (only if page converts)
Newsletter signup with clear value proposition
Podcast/media appearances (builds authority)
Testing Cadence
Review Featured section every 30 days
Track profile views → DM conversion monthly
Swap item 2 (best post) when you have a new top performer
A/B test free resource titles quarterly
Check all external links still work monthly
Update case study numbers as results improve
