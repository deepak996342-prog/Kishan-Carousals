Beyond Basic Use

Strategic Advisor
Using Claude as a
strategic advisor.
Brief it well and it thinks clearly.

'Here is my content strategy: [paste]. What are the 5 weakest assumptions? What evidence would change your recommendation?'

'Here is my proposal: [paste]. What might make them hesitate and how should I address each concern?'

AI as strategic advisor replaces expensive consultants for most service business decisions. Brief it well and it thinks clearly.

The briefing principle: The quality of the AI's strategic output is directly proportional to the quality of the context you provide. Brief it like you'd brief a senior consultant.

Custom Instructions
Custom instructions — making AI
know your business.
Eliminates the re-briefing overhead that slows most AI users.

Claude and ChatGPT support persistent context instructions. Set up a business brief: your industry, client type, service, tone, goals, constraints.

Every conversation starts with this context. The AI responds as if it already knows your business. This eliminates the re-briefing overhead that slows most AI users and produces much more relevant output.

One afternoon of setup. Permanent productivity gain.

AI-Powered Week
The AI-powered week: 10 hours
of output in 2.5.
The difference is the system, not just the tool.

Monday (30 min): Ideation session — 20 content ideas. Tuesday (45 min): Draft 5 LinkedIn posts. Wednesday (30 min): Edit, humanise, approve. Thursday (20 min): Visuals. Friday (15 min): Schedule.

Total: 2.5 hours for a complete week of content. The same output took 8-10 hours manually. The difference is the system, not just the tool.

The compound effect: 2.5 hours/week vs 10 hours/week = 390 hours saved per year. That's 10 additional working weeks of capacity.

Advanced Prompting Reference

5 techniques — when to use them and example prompt templates

Technique When to Use Example Prompt Template
Chain-of-Thought Complex problems needing structured analysis 'Think through this step by step before answering: [problem]'
Role-Play Stress-testing ideas and strategies 'You are a skeptical [role]. Challenge every assumption in: [strategy]'
Iteration Improving a draft that's 70% there 'This is good. Now make it 30% more specific, replace all generalities with examples.'
Constraint Forcing concise, clear output 'Answer in under 100 words, plain language only, no jargon.'
