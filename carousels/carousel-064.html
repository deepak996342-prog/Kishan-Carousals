Welcome Sequence
The welcome sequence —
your most important automated emails.
This sequence converts 8–15% of subscribers into enquiries.

Email 1 (immediately): deliver lead magnet + introduce yourself + set expectations.
Email 2 (day 2): your origin story — why you do what you do.
Email 3 (day 4): your methodology — how you work.
Email 4 (day 6): client case study — proof.
Email 5 (day 8): the offer — your service, clearly described.
Email 6 (day 10): objection handling. Email 7 (day 12): last chance + genuine urgency.

Re-engagement
Re-engagement sequences
— reviving cold subscribers.
A clean list of 500 engaged subscribers beats 5,000 cold ones.

After 90 days without engagement, a subscriber is 'cold.' Send a 3-email re-engagement sequence: 'Still interested?' email, 'Here's what you've missed' email, 'Last email from us' email with clear re-subscribe CTA.

Remove non-re-engagers. A clean engaged list converts dramatically better for sales and protects your deliverability.

Segmentation
Segmentation — the automation
that personalises at scale.
Segmentation doubles email revenue.

Segment by: interest (which lead magnet they downloaded), behaviour (link clicks, opens), business stage (new, growing, established).

A new business owner doesn't need the same content as a scaling agency. Each segment gets different content and offers. Segmentation doubles email revenue with the same list size.

Tool Selection
Choosing and setting up
email automation tools.
The tool matters less than the strategy.

Entry level: Mailchimp or ConvertKit (under 2,000 subscribers, free tier). Growing: ActiveCampaign or Klaviyo (advanced segmentation). Enterprise: HubSpot.

Start with what you'll actually use. A simple tool used consistently beats a complex tool abandoned after setup. You can always migrate — but you can't recover the time lost not starting.
