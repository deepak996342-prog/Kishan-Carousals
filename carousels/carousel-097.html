Generic by Default
Why AI defaults to
generic language.
Statistically common patterns — hedged, balanced, comprehensive.

AI is trained on vast amounts of content and defaults to statistically common patterns: hedged, balanced, comprehensive. Every sentence technically correct. None of it opinionated.

The tells: 'it's important to note', 'in today's digital landscape', 'leverage synergies'. These phrases signal the author didn't write this. They generated it.

Your audience can feel the difference — even if they can't name it.

The test: Read your AI draft aloud. Does it sound like you talking to a friend about your work? If not, it needs voice editing before publishing.

30-Minute Method
The 30-minute voice
training method.
Save the voice description. Use it in every future content prompt.

Collect your 10 best posts (the ones you're proudest of). In a new Claude conversation: 'These 10 posts are my writing. Analyse the patterns: vocabulary, sentence length, tone, how I use examples, how I make arguments, my personality on the page. Summarise my voice in 100 words so you can replicate it.'

Save that voice description. Use it in every future content prompt.

The 5-Edit Pass
The 5-edit pass that removes
every AI tell.
These 5 edits transform any AI draft into yours.

1. Remove all hedge words ('it's worth noting', 'it's important to remember'). 2. Cut the filler opener ('In today's rapidly changing...'). 3. Add one personal example only you would know. 4. Vary sentence length — insert one very short sentence per paragraph. 5. Add one specific detail that proves real-world experience.

These 5 edits transform any AI draft into yours.

The short sentence trick: AI writes in consistent medium-length sentences. Real writers vary. One short sentence. Then a longer one. That's rhythm.

Co-Writer vs. Ghostwriter
The co-writer vs. ghostwriter
distinction.
Your thinking shapes the skeleton. AI adds the flesh.

Ghostwriter: AI writes full draft, you publish. Results in generic output.

Co-writer: You write the key insight and structure, AI expands and polishes. Results in your thinking, AI-enhanced.

The co-writer approach produces better content because your specific expertise shapes the skeleton. AI adds the flesh. The content is genuinely yours.

Authenticity Test
The authenticity test
before every publish.
AI can produce content about your expertise. It cannot create expertise you don't have.

Ask before publishing: 'If someone asked me about this on a live call, could I defend every claim with personal experience?'

If yes: publish. If no: add the experience or remove the unsupported claim.

AI can produce content about your expertise. It cannot create expertise you don't have. This test keeps your brand honest — and your audience's trust intact.

The long-term rule: Publish only what you can defend in conversation. Your content reputation is built on the gap between what you claim and what you can back up.
