The Mindset Shift
The Repurposing
Mindset
Stop creating new content. Start extracting from what exists.

The daily content grind is built on a flawed premise: that the only way to maintain presence is to generate new ideas constantly. It isn't.

The repurposing mindset flips the question entirely. Instead of "what do I post today?", ask "what can I extract from what already exists?"

Your best post from six months ago reached 3% of your current audience. 97% of your followers have never seen it. Resharing that post — even unchanged — is new content for nearly everyone reading it.

Better still, repurposing means extracting the core insight and reformatting it for different platforms, different learning styles, and different discovery contexts. The idea stays the same. The expression multiplies.

The 10-Format Map
One LinkedIn Post →
10 Formats
Every format reaches a different person on a different platform at a different moment.

Starting from one well-performing LinkedIn post, here are the 10 formats you can extract:

1. Twitter/X thread — break the core argument into 5–7 numbered tweets.
2. Email newsletter — expand with context, a story, and a CTA.
3. Carousel — visualise the key points as a swipeable PDF/graphic.
4. Short video — record yourself explaining the concept in 60–90 seconds.
5. Quote card — pull the single best sentence. Design it. Share as image.
6. Blog post — 600–1,000 words expanding on the idea with examples.
7. Podcast angle — pitch this topic to a show host as an episode idea.
8. Instagram caption — rewrite for a visual-first audience with different hooks.
9. YouTube community post — share the concept as a poll or short reflection.
10. New LinkedIn post — opposite angle on the same core idea, 3 months later.

The Workflow
The Repurposing
Workflow
Identify → Extract → Reformat → Distribute over 2 weeks.

Repurposing without a workflow becomes chaotic. This 4-step process makes it systematic:

Step 1: Identify a high-performing piece. A post that drove unusual engagement, DMs, or shares. This is your source material. Proven resonance is a signal that the core idea is worth amplifying.

Step 2: Extract the core insight. Strip away the LinkedIn-specific formatting, the hooks, the platform context. What is the pure idea underneath? Write it in one sentence.

Step 3: Reformat for each channel. Each format gets a new introduction and new framing suited to that platform's audience and context — but the core insight stays identical.

Step 4: Publish over a 2-week window. Not all at once. Spread the repurposed formats across two weeks to maximise the reach of a single source idea.

Building the System
Building Your
Repurposing System
Source content first, distribution second — always in that order.

The most sustainable repurposing strategy is built on a content hierarchy. At the top: one long-form piece per week that serves as the source for everything else.

One long-form piece per week is the source for everything. A 1,000-word LinkedIn article. A 45-minute podcast episode. A detailed carousel. Something substantive that contains multiple ideas worth extracting.

From that one piece, you extract and distribute across the other formats throughout the week. Not starting from scratch each time — mining from what you've already created.

"Source content first, distribution second." Most creators work in reverse — they create platform-native short content without a source. This approach produces inconsistent depth and zero compounding. The source-first model creates a library of interconnected content that builds authority over time.

The 1-to-10 equation: One long-form source piece per week = 10 distribution pieces = 2 weeks of content without a single new idea.

Automation
Automating the
Repurposing Pipeline
The tools that reduce repurposing from hours to minutes.

Manual repurposing across 10 formats is still time-consuming. Automation handles the mechanical parts so you can focus on the creative adaptation.

Notion content database. A single database tracking every source piece, every derived format, and its publishing status. You can see gaps instantly — "I have the LinkedIn post and email but haven't done the carousel yet."

Zapier cross-posting. Automate the distribution of certain formats to certain platforms. New blog post → automatically notified to email list, for example.

AI for rewriting for different audiences. Give Claude or ChatGPT your source piece and ask it to rewrite for a Twitter audience, an email audience, or an Instagram audience. The core idea is yours. The reformatting takes minutes.

Schedulers for distribution. Buffer, Hypefury, or Later for pre-loading your 2-week distribution window.
