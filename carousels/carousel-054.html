The 5-Second Test
The 5-second bio test.
Three questions your bio must answer instantly.

A visitor to your profile has 5 seconds before they decide to follow or leave. Your bio must answer in that time: what do you do, who do you help, and what should they do next. If any of these is unclear or missing, you're losing followers who would have become clients. Test your own bio right now — can you answer all three in under 5 seconds?

Name Field Hack
The name field hack that gets
you discovered.
Instagram's search indexes your name field, not your bio.

Instead of 'Sarah Johnson' write 'Sarah Johnson | LinkedIn Coach'. Now you appear in searches for 'LinkedIn Coach'. This single change increases profile visits from search for free. Keywords in the name field = free organic discovery. This is the most underused Instagram SEO tactic for service business owners.

Bio Formula
Writing a bio that converts
visitors to followers.
Under 150 characters. Specific. Clear. Action-oriented.

Formula: [What you do] + [Who you help] + [The result they get] + [CTA]. Example: 'I help founders create content that generates leads. 30-min free audit ↓'. Under 150 characters. Specific. Clear. Action-oriented. No fluff, no emojis unless they add meaning. Every word must earn its place. Cut anything that doesn't clarify or convert.

Link Strategy
The link-in-bio strategy.
One link. Not a link tree with 10 options.

Decision paralysis kills conversion. Your link should go to a single high-value offer: a free resource (builds email list), a booking page (books calls), or a specific landing page for your primary service. Test different destinations quarterly and measure which converts. One clear path outperforms ten confusing options every single time.

Visual First Impression
Profile photo, highlights, and grid —
the visual first impression.
Visual consistency = professional credibility.

Profile photo: professional, well-lit, simple background. Grid: consistent color palette and style — not perfectly curated, but recognisably yours. First 6 posts in grid are part of your bio experience. Highlights: labelled clearly with matching cover images. Visual consistency = professional credibility. Prospects judge your business by your grid before they read a single word.
