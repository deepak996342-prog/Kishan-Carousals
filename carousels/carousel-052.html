Distribution Advantage
Why Reels reach non-followers —
the distribution advantage.
Reels are Instagram's built-in growth engine for creators.

Unlike feed posts that reach mainly your followers, Reels get pushed to the Explore page and Reels tab. This is Instagram's algorithm saying 'we'll find an audience for you.' A consultant with 500 followers can get 50,000 Reel views on the right content. The opportunity cost of not doing Reels is massive reach left on the table.

4 Formats
The 4 Reels formats that
build authority.
Rotate all four for a well-rounded authority presence.

1. Quick tip (under 30s): 'The fastest way to [result].'
2. Myth-bust: 'Stop believing [wrong advice].'
3. Before/after reveal — show a real transformation.
4. Behind-the-scenes of your work — the process clients pay for.
Each format positions you differently. Rotate all four for a well-rounded authority presence.

Scroll-Stopping Hooks
Reels hooks that stop the scroll
in 2 seconds.
Your hook is the first frame + first spoken word.

Never start with 'Hey guys.' Start with the punchline: 'Most [your client type] make this $5,000 mistake.' The scroll stops when the opening creates instant recognition of a problem they have. Your hook is a promise. The rest of the Reel is the payoff. If the hook doesn't land, nothing else matters.

Technical Guide
Captions, hashtags, and audio —
the technical guide.
The details that separate average Reels from great ones.

85% of Reels are watched without sound. Captions aren't optional — they're essential. Keep captions in the middle-bottom, not overlapping faces. Hashtags: 3-5 specific ones, not 30 generic. Original audio slightly outperforms trending audio for discoverability in professional niches. Every technical decision compounds over time.

Repurposing System
Repurposing LinkedIn content
into Reels — the 10-minute method.
One idea becomes content on two platforms.

Take your best performing LinkedIn post. Extract the 3 key points. Record yourself explaining each in 15 seconds. Add captions and a simple hook. This repurposing system means one idea becomes content on two platforms with minimal extra work. Your best LinkedIn insights are already validated. Now give them visual reach.
