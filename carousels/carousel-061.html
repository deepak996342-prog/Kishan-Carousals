The 5-Email Framework

List Building
Building your list from zero
— the ethical bribe.
A lead magnet valuable enough to trade an email address for.

Templates, calculators, guides, checklists, mini-courses. Rule: if you wouldn't pay $10 for it, they won't trade their email.

The specificity principle: 'LinkedIn Profile Template for [niche]' outperforms 'LinkedIn Tips PDF' 3-to-1 in conversions.

The test: Would your ideal client mention this lead magnet to a colleague? If yes — it's specific enough to convert.

Email Frequency
The frequency debate —
settled.
Consistency matters more than frequency.

Weekly email = 80% retention. Daily email = 50% retention (unless your content is exceptional). Biweekly = too easy to forget who you are.

For most service businesses: weekly is optimal. Send on Tuesday or Wednesday morning. The subscribers who stay through 10 emails become clients.

Email Metrics
The metrics that actually
matter for service businesses.
Benchmarks for professional services email.

Open rate benchmark: 25–30% for professional services (not 'good' — standard). Click rate: 3–5% is solid. Unsubscribe rate: under 0.5% per email is healthy.

But the real metric: replies. An email that generates 10 genuine replies from ideal prospects is more valuable than 1,000 opens.
