Why newsletters outperform

Social content lifespan: 48 hours. Newsletters: weeks.

The compounding effect: an email list doubles in value every 18 months when nurtured properly. No social platform offers that trajectory.

Newsletter Anatomy
The anatomy of a newsletter
people actually read.
One insight per issue beats five mediocre insights every time.

Subject line → preview text → personal opener (1 sentence) → one main insight or story → practical takeaway → CTA (one, clear) → sign-off.

The single biggest newsletter mistake: trying to include everything. One great insight that changes how someone thinks is worth a hundred forgettable bullet lists.

Unique Angle
Finding your newsletter's
unique angle.
The contrarian angle consistently expressed builds a loyal audience.

There are thousands of 'marketing tips' newsletters. The newsletters that grow are those with a clear POV (point of view).

What do you believe that most people in your industry get wrong? That contrarian angle, consistently expressed, builds a loyal audience that can't get that perspective anywhere else.

The POV test: Can someone read 3 issues of your newsletter and clearly articulate what you stand for? If not, sharpen your angle.

5 Growth Channels
Growing your newsletter
— the 5 channels that work.
Each channel compounds over time.

1. LinkedIn content with newsletter CTA.
2. Instagram link-in-bio.

4. Guest newsletter swaps with complementary creators.

Monetisation
Monetising your newsletter
for service businesses.
The real revenue is in the relationship.

Primary: generate leads for your service. Secondary: sponsored issues (once you reach 2,000+ subscribers). Tertiary: paid newsletter tier.

But the real revenue is in the relationship. A reader who's been receiving your newsletter for 6 months trusts you at a level that no social follower matches.
