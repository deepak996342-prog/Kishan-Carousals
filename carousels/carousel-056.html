The 2025 Reality
The truth about hashtags
in 2025.
Two functions remain valuable — everything else is noise.

Instagram's own research shows that more hashtags don't mean more reach. The algorithm now uses content understanding more than hashtags. But hashtags still serve two functions: community discovery (people who follow hashtags see your post) and SEO (searchable). Use them strategically, not as a spam tactic.

3-Tier System
The 3-tier hashtag system.
Use all three tiers in every post. Total: 9-13 tags maximum.

Tier 1 (1-3 tags): broad topic, 1M+ posts. Large audiences but high competition.
Tier 2 (3-5 tags): medium niche, 100K-1M posts. Best for discoverability.
Tier 3 (3-5 tags): micro-niche, under 100K posts. Highest chance of reaching your ideal audience.
Use all three tiers in every post. Total: 9-13 tags maximum.

Finding the Right Tags
Finding hashtags your ideal
clients actually follow.
Better to reach 100 ideal clients than 10,000 wrong people.

Search your ideal client's profile. What hashtags do they use? What hashtags do they follow? Start there. Better to reach 100 ideal clients with specific hashtags than 10,000 wrong people with popular ones. The goal of hashtags is qualified reach, not vanity reach. Specificity converts; popularity just inflates numbers.

Caption vs. Comment
Hashtags in caption vs. first
comment — what the data shows.
What doesn't work: stuffing 30 generic hashtags into every post.

Caption hashtags perform slightly better for immediate reach. First comment hashtags look cleaner. Either works. What doesn't work: stuffing 30 generic hashtags into every post. The algorithm reads this as spam behaviour and reduces your reach. Quality and relevance over quantity — always. 9-13 strategic tags outperform 30 random ones.

Monthly Audit
The monthly hashtag audit.
Build a rotating hashtag bank of 40-50 tested tags.

Check which hashtags are driving profile visits (Instagram Insights shows this). Remove any hashtag that's attracting irrelevant followers or zero engagement. Test 5 new hashtags every 30 days. Build a hashtag bank of 40-50 rotating tags that you cycle through. This prevents algorithm fatigue from seeing the same tags on every post from your account.
