What Technical SEO Is
Not optional, but not as
complex as it sounds.
The fundamentals: fast, mobile, crawlable, error-free.

Technical SEO is not optional for serious websites — but it's also not as complex as agencies make it sound. The fundamentals are four things: your site loads fast, it works on mobile, Google can crawl it, and your pages have no critical errors.
Most service business websites fail on 2–3 of these basics. Fixing them doesn't require a developer — it requires awareness and an afternoon. After that, focus entirely on content. Technical SEO is the floor. Content is the ceiling.

4 Critical Issues
The 4 technical issues killing
most service business websites.
Run Google Search Console. It shows you all four for free.

1. Slow loading: 40% of visitors leave if a page takes over 3 seconds. This directly destroys rankings and conversions.
2. Not mobile-friendly: 60% of searches are on mobile. Google uses mobile-first indexing — your mobile site IS your site.
3. Broken links (404 errors): destroys trust, ranking power, and user experience simultaneously.
4. Missing meta descriptions: reduces search click-through rate — visitors choose competitors who describe their content clearly.
Fix these four and you've solved 90% of technical SEO.

Google Search Console
The free tool you should
be checking every month.
Setup takes 10 minutes. The data is invaluable.

Google Search Console shows you: which queries bring people to your site (invaluable for content ideas), pages with errors, mobile usability issues, and core web vital scores — all for free.
Check it monthly. The 'Performance' tab shows your fastest-growing pages — double down on those topics. The 'Coverage' tab shows crawl errors to fix. The 'Mobile Usability' tab shows exactly which pages fail on mobile.
This single free tool contains more actionable SEO insight than most paid platforms.

First action: Go to Performance → Queries. Sort by 'Impressions'. These are the searches your site nearly appears for — write content targeting the top 10.

Site Structure
The architecture that helps
Google understand your site.
Every page reachable in 3 clicks. Clean URLs always.

Your site structure: homepage → service pages → blog posts / case studies → contact page. Every page should be reachable within 3 clicks from the homepage.
Use a simple, descriptive URL structure: yoursite.com/services/linkedin-strategy — not yoursite.com/page/id=2847. Clean URLs get indexed faster and communicate topic relevance to Google.
Your navigation should reflect your services. A visitor — and Google's crawler — should understand what you do and who you serve within 10 seconds of landing on your homepage.

Schema Markup
The technical detail that gets
you rich snippets in search.
Doubled search result size. 20–30% higher click-through rate.

Schema markup tells Google what your content is: FAQ, review, service, person. Adding FAQ schema to relevant pages can create rich snippets — expanded search results that take up twice the space.
Rich snippets get 20–30% higher click-through rates than standard results because they're visually larger and show more information. Google's Structured Data Markup Helper creates schema for free — no coding required.
This is one of the few technical SEO tactics that visibly increases traffic without changing your ranking position.

Quick win: Add FAQ schema to your top 3 blog posts. Each FAQ answer becomes a visible snippet in search results within 4–6 weeks.
