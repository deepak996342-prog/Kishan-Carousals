What Matters
The metrics that actually matter
for service businesses on Instagram.
Followers are a trailing metric — focus on intent signals.

For lead generation, ignore: follower growth rate, impressions, profile visits. Focus on: reach (how many new people), saves (content value), story replies (relationship depth), DMs received (conversion intent). Followers are a trailing metric — they follow after enough value is delivered. Lead with signals of intent.

Audience Insights
Audience insights — understanding
who's watching.
The 60/40 rule for audience demographics.

Instagram shows your audience demographics: age, location, active hours. Most valuable insight: what time is your audience most active? Post within 1 hour of that window. Secondary insight: the 60/40 rule — if more than 60% of your audience is the wrong demographic, your content positioning needs adjusting. Data shows you what words alone cannot.

Weekly Review
Post performance analysis —
the weekly 10-minute review.
Sort by saves and by reach — both lists are necessary.

Sort your last month's posts by saves. The top 3 are your best content. Study what they have in common. Sort by reach. The top 3 are your best discovery content. These two lists often don't overlap — that's normal. Both types are necessary: saves deepen relationships, reach expands your audience. You need both engines running.

Story Analytics
Story analytics — the
intimacy metric.
Story completion rate is the key metric.

Story completion rate (% who watch all slides) is the key metric. Under 70%: your stories are too long or not interesting enough. 80%+: strong. The drop-off slide tells you exactly where you lose viewers. Cut everything after that slide in future series. Your Stories analytics are a direct line to understanding what your warmest audience actually wants.

Monthly Dashboard
Building a monthly
analytics dashboard.
After 3 months, patterns emerge — let data guide your experiments.

Track these 5 numbers monthly: reach growth %, average saves per post, story completion rate, DMs received, profile visits → link clicks conversion rate. Plot them on a simple spreadsheet. After 3 months, patterns emerge. Let data guide your content experiments. You stop guessing and start knowing what your audience responds to. Data is your content compass.
