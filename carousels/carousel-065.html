Lead Magnet Strategy
Lead magnet strategy —
creating something worth the email.
If you'd tweet about it, it's good enough as a lead magnet.

The worst lead magnets: generic ebooks, '10 tips' PDFs, vague checklists.

The best: specific tools (templates, calculators, scripts), compressed frameworks (one-page cheat sheets), actionable guides (step-by-step for a specific outcome).

Rule: if you'd tweet about it as a piece of content, it's good enough as a lead magnet.

LinkedIn Pipeline
The LinkedIn-to-email pipeline
that generates 50–200 subscribers per post.
One high-value post per week = consistent list growth.

Post valuable LinkedIn content → add 'Want the full template? Comment TEMPLATE' → DM commenters the download page → they subscribe.

This method generates 50–200 new subscribers per LinkedIn post for accounts with 2,000+ connections. One high-value post per week produces consistent, compounding list growth.

The flywheel: more subscribers → better content feedback → better LinkedIn content → more subscribers.

Landing Page
Optimising your sign-up page
for conversion.
Optimised pages convert 30–50% of visitors.

Your landing page has one job: convert visitors to subscribers. Remove navigation. Remove distractions.

Headline: specific outcome. Sub: what's inside. CTA button: 'Send me the [specific thing]' not 'Subscribe.' Social proof: number of subscribers or testimonial. Test: remove one element at a time.

Referral Loop
The referral loop — getting
subscribers to bring subscribers.
The best source of new subscribers is existing subscribers.

Add to every email: 'Find this valuable? Forward to one person who'd benefit.' Add a referral link (SparkLoop or beehiiv). When a subscriber brings 3 new subscribers, reward them with a bonus resource.

They self-select the audience quality — referred subscribers open, click, and convert at 2–3× the rate of paid acquisition.
