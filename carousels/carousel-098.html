A/B Test Analysis
A/B test analysis with AI —
extracting useful insight from small data.
AI extracts useful insight from small datasets that humans find inconclusive.

Run a test: 2 post hooks, 2 email subjects, 2 thumbnails. After 72 hours, paste results: 'Here are 2 version results: [data]. Which performed better? Why? What does this reveal about my audience's preferences? What should I test next?'

AI extracts useful insight from small datasets that humans find inconclusive. Every test, however small, teaches you something specific about your audience.
