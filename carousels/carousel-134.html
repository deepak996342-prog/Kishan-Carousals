Root Cause of Blank Screen
It's rarely lack of ideas.
It's lack of capture.
Uncaptured ideas don't exist when you need them.

You have ideas constantly. In the shower. During client calls. When reading an article. When you make a mistake and realise something.

The problem isn't generation — it's capture. Those ideas evaporate in minutes if you don't catch them. A blank screen on Wednesday isn't a creativity problem. It's a Monday capture failure.

The fix: carry a capture tool everywhere. Notes app. Voice memo. Notion mobile. The moment an idea surfaces — capture it. One sentence. That's enough. 30 captures per month = never facing a blank screen.

The capture habit: After every client call, write one thing that surprised you or confirmed something you believed. 30 seconds. One sentence. That's a post idea.

Triggers 1–3 · Experience Mining
Your last 7 days contain
at least 3 post ideas.
Mine your own experience — it's original by definition.

Trigger 1: The last conversation that surprised you. When did someone say something in the last week that shifted your thinking? What surprised you about their perspective? That surprise is a post.

Trigger 2: A mistake this week that taught you something. What went wrong? What would you do differently? The lesson is worth sharing.

Trigger 3: A belief you've changed. What did you used to think that you no longer think? What changed your mind? Before/after belief shifts are some of the most engaging posts on LinkedIn.

Triggers 4–6 · Audience-Centred
Your audience is asking
you questions every day.
Their questions are your best content brief.

Trigger 4: The most common question you get asked. If someone asks you the same thing more than twice, it's a post. The question is the proof that the audience wants the answer.

Trigger 5: The misconception you correct most often. What do clients consistently misunderstand about your field? Correcting it publicly is service and positioning simultaneously.

Trigger 6: What clients always underestimate. What do new clients fail to appreciate about the complexity or importance of what you do? Making that visible educates your future clients before they hire you.

Triggers 7–9 · Industry Lens
Your perspective on your
industry is unique content.
Credibility comes from having opinions, not just information.

Trigger 7: A trend you're skeptical of. What's everyone excited about in your field that you think is overrated or misunderstood? Contrarian takes drive more discussion than consensus.

Trigger 8: Advice that gets repeated that you think is wrong. What's the received wisdom in your niche that you disagree with? Disagreeing publicly (respectfully and with reasoning) signals expertise and courage.

Trigger 9: What new people need to hear first. If someone just entered your field or started their journey, what's the most important thing to understand? That lesson is always useful.

Triggers 10–12 · Contrast
Comparison posts work
because contrast creates clarity.
The gap between A and B is always more interesting than either alone.

Trigger 10: Then vs. now (your journey). What did you think 3 years ago that you'd completely disagree with now? Your evolution is evidence of expertise and authenticity.

Trigger 11: The difference between A and B that nobody explains. Two concepts that people confuse — strategy vs. tactics, authority vs. fame, engagement vs. reach. Explain the distinction clearly and concisely.

Trigger 12: What you wish someone told you. What would have saved you months of confusion if you'd heard it at the start? That lesson is gold for everyone who is where you were.
