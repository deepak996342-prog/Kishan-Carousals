Streams 1–2 · Fastest Money
Start where the money
arrives quickest.
High margin, immediate — but limited by your hours.

Stream 1: 1:1 Consulting. The fastest path from LinkedIn follower to client revenue. High margin (you keep nearly everything you charge), immediate (a DM can become a paid call this week), and the best validation engine — if people pay for your time, your expertise is real. The ceiling is your available hours, but the floor is very low.

Stream 2: Done-for-you work. You execute something on behalf of your client — writing, strategy, systems, campaigns. Requires a clear scope and a strong proposal process to avoid scope creep. Higher value per engagement than consulting, but more time-intensive. Productize the deliverable to keep delivery efficient.

To start: State clearly in your profile headline what you offer. Then post content that demonstrates that you're the best at it. DMs follow naturally.

Streams 3–4 · Leverage Time
Same time input,
more revenue output.
This is where income starts to scale beyond your hours.

Stream 3: Group coaching / cohort programs. Deliver the same outcome to 5–20 people simultaneously. You prepare once, deliver to many. Pricing is typically 40–60% of 1:1 rates, but revenue per hour of delivery can exceed 1:1 significantly. Requires a proven outcome and a warm audience ready to commit to a time-bounded program.

Stream 4: Advisory retainers. Clients pay a monthly fee for recurring access to your thinking — typically 2–4 hours per month. Recurring revenue, low preparation overhead, long client relationships. Best for experienced practitioners with a clear domain where ongoing guidance creates continuous value. Advisory retainers are the most lifestyle-friendly revenue stream available.

Streams 5–6 · Productized
Package your knowledge.
Sell it while you sleep.
Create once. Deliver infinitely.

Stream 5: Digital downloads. Templates, swipe files, guides, prompt libraries, frameworks as PDFs. Priced £25–£97 typically. No delivery overhead — purchase triggers automatic delivery. Your LinkedIn content is the marketing engine. The most accessible entry point to passive income.

Stream 6: Online courses. Higher production investment upfront, higher price point. A well-structured course solving one specific problem can command £297–£997+. Requires a proven outcome and testimonials to sustain sales. The key mistake: building before validating. Pre-sell to 5 people before recording a single module. If 5 people pay — build it. If not — find out why first.

Productization rule: If you've explained the same thing to 3 different clients, it belongs in a product. Stop giving it away free and start packaging it.

Stream 7 · Audience as Asset
Your audience itself
becomes the product.
Recurring revenue from community and brand partnerships.

Paid community or newsletter. Your most engaged followers pay for deeper access — exclusive content, peer connection, Q&A access, early releases. Monthly recurring revenue that compounds with retention. Requires a critical mass of highly engaged followers before launch, but once established it is the most predictable income stream available to a creator.

Sponsorships and brand partnerships. Brands pay to reach your audience through your content. Requires scale (typically 10,000+ followers), niche alignment, and strong engagement metrics. Revenue per post or per campaign. This stream rewards audience quality over quantity — a 5,000-follower niche account often outperforms a 50,000-follower generalist for relevant brands.

Building a Revenue Stack
One stream validates.
A stack multiplies.
The order matters more than the speed.

The temptation is to launch multiple revenue streams simultaneously. This usually means none of them get the focus they need to work. The proven sequence is: start with 1:1 (validates your expertise and generates immediate cash), add a digital product (leverages your IP without adding delivery time), build a community or cohort (adds recurring and leveraged income), and layer courses at scale.

Each stage funds the next. The 1:1 work generates cash. The digital product generates passive revenue. The community generates recurring income. The course generates scale. By the time you have all four, you have a resilient revenue base that doesn't collapse when one stream slows down.

Revenue stack rule: Don't add a new stream until the previous one is generating consistent monthly revenue. Depth before breadth.
