SSI Explained
What SSI Measures
(0–100 Scale)
Four pillars worth 25 points each — totalling 100.

LinkedIn's Social Selling Index (SSI) is a score from 0–100 that measures your effectiveness on the platform across four dimensions. The higher your score, the broader your organic content distribution.

LinkedIn's own data shows that users with an SSI above 70 have 45% more opportunities created than those below 30. Your score updates daily and reflects your activity over the previous 90 days.

You can view your score at linkedin.com/sales/ssi — it's free and public. Check it now before you read the rest of this.

Pillar 1
Establish Your
Professional Brand
25 points — the foundation everything else sits on.

This pillar measures how well your profile presents you as a credible expert and how actively you create content.

Complete your profile to 100%. All sections filled, including skills, certifications, and a strong summary. LinkedIn's algorithm actively penalises incomplete profiles.

Post original content 3+ times per week. LinkedIn measures content creation frequency directly in this pillar. Sharing others' content doesn't count — only original posts.

Optimise your headline for search. Include your primary keyword naturally. This lifts your profile in LinkedIn search results, which increases views, which increases pillar score.

Pillars 2 & 3
Find the Right People
& Engage With Insights
50 combined points — proactive and reactive activity both count.

Pillar 2 — Find the Right People (25 pts): Use LinkedIn Search with filters to find your ideal clients and peers. Save searches. View profiles of prospects. Add them to saved lead lists if you use Sales Navigator. LinkedIn tracks whether you're actively prospecting or passively waiting.

Pillar 3 — Engage With Insights (25 pts): Comment on content shared by prospects and industry leaders — substantive comments, not just "Great post." Share relevant articles with your own commentary. React to content your connections share. This pillar rewards active network participation, not broadcasting.

Pillar 4
Build Strong
Relationships
25 points — the pillar most creators completely ignore.

This pillar measures the quality and depth of your network interactions — not just size.

Personalised connection requests. A custom note dramatically increases acceptance rates. LinkedIn tracks whether you're connecting strategically or mass-blasting. Quality connections signal intent.

Respond to DMs within 24 hours. Responsiveness is measured. Leaving messages unread for days hurts this pillar.

Follow up with new connections. A simple message 48 hours after connecting — no pitch, just genuine interest — moves connections from pending to active. This is what LinkedIn calls "relationship building."

Weekly Routine
The 30-Minute Weekly
SSI Improvement Routine
Specific daily actions that move all four pillars simultaneously.

Monday (5 min): Check your SSI score at linkedin.com/sales/ssi. Note which pillar dropped. That's your focus for the week.

Tuesday–Thursday (5 min/day): Post one piece of original content. Spend 5 minutes commenting on 3–5 posts from people in your target audience. This builds pillars 1 and 3 simultaneously.

Friday (10 min): Connect with 10 new people using personalised notes. View 10–15 profiles of potential connections. This builds pillars 2 and 4.

Ongoing: Reply to every DM same day. Respond to every comment within 2 hours of posting.
