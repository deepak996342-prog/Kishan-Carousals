Curiosity Gap
Creating urgency
without clickbait.
The curiosity gap: the space between what they know and what they want to know.

The best subject lines create a gap that can only be closed by opening the email.

Critical rule: the email MUST deliver what the subject line promises. Clickbait destroys trust faster than anything else in email marketing. You get the open once. You lose the subscriber forever.

The test: Read your subject line. Then read your email. Does the email close the loop? If not, rewrite the subject line — not the email.

A/B Testing
A/B testing subject lines
— the 20-minute weekly process.
How to double your open rate in 4 months.

Split your list in half. Send version A to 50%, version B to 50%. Wait 4 hours. The higher-open-rate version 'wins.' Send the winner to everyone who didn't receive either.

After 4 months, you have a database of what your audience responds to. This doubles your open rate — with zero additional effort beyond the initial setup.

Deliverability Killers
Subject line mistakes that
kill your deliverability.
These trigger spam filters and teach your audience to ignore you.

ALL CAPS. Excessive punctuation!!!. Spam trigger words: 'free', 'guaranteed', 'limited time'. Subject lines over 60 characters (cut off on mobile). Starting with 'Re:' or 'Fwd:' as fake replies.

These don't just reduce open rates — they trigger spam filters and permanently damage your sender reputation.

Subject Line Swipe File

6 ready-to-use subject line examples

The Question
"Are you making this content mistake?"
Creates self-doubt and curiosity — powerful for professional audiences.
The Number
"5 subject lines that get 50% open rates"
Specific numbers signal concrete, scannable value inside.
The Contrarian
"Why I stopped posting daily"
Contradicts common advice — stands out in a crowded inbox.
The Personal
"I almost didn't send this"
Vulnerability creates human connection and intrigue instantly.
Ultra-Specific
"The one LinkedIn change that doubled my leads"
Specificity signals this is real, tested information — not generic advice.
The Open Loop
"I tried something different last week..."
The ellipsis creates an unresolved pattern the brain needs to close.
Test two formulas against each other every send. Over time, your swipe file becomes your personal playbook — built on your audience's actual behaviour.
