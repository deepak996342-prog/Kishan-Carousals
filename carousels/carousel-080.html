Vanity Metrics
The vanity SEO metrics that sound
important but aren't.
These impress in agency reports but don't connect to revenue.

Domain authority (third-party metric, not Google's). Alexa rank (discontinued). Raw number of backlinks without quality context. Keyword rankings for terms with zero business intent.

These metrics impress in agency reports but don't connect to revenue. For service businesses: the only SEO metrics worth tracking are those tied directly to leads and enquiries.

The 5 Real Metrics
The 5 SEO metrics that
predict business results.
These five, tracked monthly, tell the complete story.

1. Organic sessions — total visits from search. The baseline.
2. Organic leads — form fills and calls via search. The money metric.
3. Click-through rate from search — how compelling your titles are.
4. Rankings for target keywords — your most commercially valuable queries.
5. Pages generating traffic — which content is working.

These five, tracked monthly, tell the complete story.

GA4 & Search Console
Setting up GA4 and Search Console
correctly.
Set both up before publishing a single article.

Google Analytics 4: free, tracks what visitors do on your site.
Google Search Console: free, tracks how Google sees your site.

Together: Search Console answers 'who's finding me', Analytics answers 'what are they doing.'

Set both up before publishing a single article. Connect them in Search Console settings for unified reporting.

Both are free. There is no reason to not have them installed. Check them both within 30 minutes of publishing any new content.

Monthly SEO Review
The monthly SEO review —
30 minutes, maximum value.
Consistent monthly reviews compound over 12 months.

0–10 min: Search Console — top queries, new keyword opportunities, pages with declining CTR.
10–20 min: Analytics — organic traffic trend, top landing pages, conversion rate from organic.
20–30 min: decisions — update one declining page, add content for one new keyword, fix one technical issue.

Consistent monthly reviews compound over 12 months.

SEO Metrics Dashboard

5 metrics — how to find each and targets by month

Metric 1
Organic Sessions
Found in: GA4 → Acquisition → Organic Search
3-month target: 200–500/month
6-month target: 500–1,500/month
12-month target: 1,500–5,000/month
Metric 2
Organic Leads
Found in: GA4 → Conversions (organic source)
3-month target: 1–3 leads/month
6-month target: 3–8 leads/month
12-month target: 7–25 leads/month
Metric 3
Click-Through Rate
Found in: Search Console → Performance → CTR
3-month target: establish baseline CTR
6-month target: improve title tags for <3% CTR pages
12-month target: 3–8% CTR on target keywords
Track these five metrics monthly without exception. Consistency of measurement is what turns SEO from guesswork into a growth engine.
