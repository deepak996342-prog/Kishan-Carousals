Strategy 01 · Decision Maker Content
Write for the person
who signs the contract.
C-suite buyers read differently from practitioners — adjust your content accordingly.

Most B2B content is written for practitioners — the people who use products. But decision makers read for strategic outcomes: revenue impact, risk reduction, competitive advantage, and team efficiency.

Reframe your content lens. Instead of "How to use [tool] better," write "Why [tool category] is becoming a strategic lever for growth-stage companies." Same expertise, radically different audience — and a much shorter path to the conversation that matters.

Filter: Before posting, ask: "Would a CFO or COO find this useful?" If not, reframe it.

Strategy 02 · Case Study Posts
The content format
that converts best in B2B.
Before/after transformation posts build trust faster than any other format.

A case study post follows this structure: Situation → Problem → What we did → Measurable result. It's short (under 400 words), specific (real numbers), and ends with a takeaway applicable to similar companies.

This format works because it's proof wrapped in a story. Decision makers see themselves in the "before" and want the "after." It's not a pitch — it's a demonstration that you've solved the exact problem they're facing for someone just like them.

Template: "A [industry] client came to us with [problem]. In [timeframe], we achieved [result] by doing [approach]. Here's what we learned."

Strategy 03 · Inbound Pipeline
Build pipeline without
a single cold call.
Content that teaches attracts buyers who are already pre-sold.

The old B2B playbook: cold call, cold email, chase. The new playbook: publish insights that attract buyers who are already looking for what you offer.

When your content consistently answers the questions your buyers are Googling or thinking about, they find you — not the other way around. They arrive in your DMs with context, trust, and intent. These conversations close 3–5× faster than cold-sourced leads because the trust-building happened in public before the private conversation began.

Shift: Every post you write is a pre-qualification tool. Write content that filters in your ideal buyer and filters out everyone else.

Strategy 04 · The Retargeting Effect
Show up in their feed
before they're ready to buy.
Consistent visibility creates familiarity — and familiarity drives preference.

B2B purchase cycles are long — often 3–18 months. Decision makers are researching for months before they act. If you're consistently in their feed during that research phase, you become the default option when they're finally ready.

LinkedIn content acts as organic retargeting. Every post that surfaces in front of a potential buyer reinforces your expertise and keeps you top-of-mind. By the time they're ready to buy, they feel like they already know you — because in a sense, they do.

Rule of 7: Decision makers typically need 7+ touchpoints with your brand before they take action. Consistent posting creates those touchpoints passively.

Strategy 05 · Converting to Calls
Turn content views
into booked conversations.
Content builds trust. But you still have to make the ask.

Views and followers don't pay your bills. Conversations do. The bridge between content and revenue is an intentional conversion mechanism — a CTA, a DM follow-up to commenters, or a periodic offer in your content.

When someone engages with your content — likes it, comments, shares it — they've raised their hand. That's the moment to start a warm conversation. Not a pitch, but a genuine outreach based on their specific comment or reaction. This turns passive content reach into active pipeline.

System: Review post engagers 24 hours after posting. Personally message 5–10 who fit your ICP. Reference their comment. Start a conversation.
