5 Core Metrics
The 5 Metrics That
Actually Drive Decisions
Track these weekly. Ignore everything else until you've mastered these.

Impressions: How many times your content was shown. This is your reach signal — are you growing or shrinking?

Engagement Rate: (Reactions + Comments + Saves + Shares) ÷ Impressions. Above 3% is good. Above 6% is excellent. This measures resonance.

Followers Gained: Net new followers per post. This tells you which content attracts your ideal audience.

Profile Views: High-intent signal. Someone who viewed your profile after seeing a post is considering reaching out.

CTR (Click-Through Rate): How often people click your links. Critical for driving traffic to your lead magnet or services.

Post-Level Analytics
What Each Post Metric
Is Actually Telling You
Four engagement types — each measures something different.

Reactions measure emotional resonance. High reactions, low comments = content people agree with but don't feel compelled to discuss.

Comments measure conversation value. Comment-heavy posts have the strongest algorithm signal — LinkedIn distributes them further because they generate ongoing engagement.

Shares measure amplification. When someone shares your post, they're saying "my network needs to see this." These are your most powerful advocacy signals.

Saves are perhaps the most underrated metric. A save means "I want to return to this later" — it signals that your content is reference-worthy, not just scroll-worthy.

Audience Analytics
Why Audience Demographics
Are the Most Underused Data
You can't serve an audience you haven't accurately identified.

Creator Mode unlocks detailed follower demographics including industry, job function, seniority, company size, and geography. Most creators check this once and forget it exists. Review it monthly.

The critical question: Are the right people following you? If you're a B2B consultant targeting VP-level decision-makers and your audience skews heavily toward junior marketers or students, your content positioning is off — no matter how good your engagement rate looks.

Use audience demographics to refine your content focus, not validate your ego. The goal is the right 5,000 followers, not the wrong 50,000.

Monthly check: Does your top industry follower match your ideal client industry? If not, your content focus needs adjusting.

Content Patterns
Finding the Patterns
That Tell You What to Post Next
Data removes guesswork. Most creators never look for patterns.

After 20+ posts, your analytics start to reveal patterns that your intuition cannot. Look for:

Which format drives the most reach? Compare text-only vs. carousel vs. video impression averages. You will almost certainly have a clear winner — most people are surprised by the result.

Which topic cluster gets the most comments? Comments signal that the topic creates conversation. Double down on the topic that gets people talking — not the one you find easiest to write about.

What posting time consistently outperforms? Compare engagement rates by time of day over 30+ posts. Find your window and protect it.

Monthly Review
The Monthly 30-Minute
Analytics Review That Changes Everything
30 minutes once a month beats checking daily and acting on noise.

Step 1 — Review your 5 KPIs vs. the previous 4 weeks. Are impressions up or down? Engagement rate up or down? Follower growth trend up or down? You need a direction before you can change direction.

Step 2 — Identify your top post. Which post had the highest engagement rate this month? Open it. Read it again. Ask: what made this work? Hook? Topic? Format? Specific story?

Step 3 — Analyse why it worked. Write your hypothesis in one sentence. This is your experiment for next month.

Step 4 — Plan next month accordingly. Create more content in the format, on the topic, with the hook style that won last month.
