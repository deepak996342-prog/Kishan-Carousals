The False Binary
The false binary that's slowing

Human insight + AI production + human curation. Remove any one and quality collapses.

'Should I use AI or write everything myself?' is the wrong question.

The right question: what role should human expertise play and what role should AI production play?

The creators winning in 2025 use both: human insight + AI production + human curation. Each element is non-negotiable. Remove any one and quality collapses.

The three-part formula: Human insight (irreplaceable) + AI production (fast) + human curation (quality control). All three. Every piece of content.

The Process
The Human + AI content process —
step by step.
4x output at the same quality level.

Step 1 (Human): Identify the insight, experience, or opinion worth sharing. Step 2 (AI): Draft structure, add context, polish language. Step 3 (Human): Add specific detail, edit voice, remove AI tells. Step 4 (AI): Repurpose for other formats. Step 5 (Human): Review and approve all final content.

This process produces 4x output at the same quality level.
