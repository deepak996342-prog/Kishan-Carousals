Ethics
The ethics of AI video —
where to draw the line.
AI video is a production tool, not a deception tool.

Best practices: Use AI to produce content that accurately represents your ideas. Disclose AI-generated avatars when relevant. Never fabricate testimonials or misrepresent clients. Never create content you wouldn't be comfortable owning publicly.

The ideas are yours. AI is the production layer.

AI video is a production efficiency tool — not a credibility substitute. The moment it's used to deceive, it destroys what it was supposed to build.

The authenticity test: 'If someone asked me about this on a live call, could I defend every claim with personal experience?' If yes — publish. If no — edit first.
