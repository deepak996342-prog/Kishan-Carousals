The Core Problem
Why most people use AI for
content creation completely wrong.
Generic input = generic output. Every time.

They type 'write me a LinkedIn post about content marketing' and get generic, lifeless output. Then they complain that AI can't write like them. The problem isn't the AI — it's the prompt.

AI is only as specific as your instructions. The best AI users treat prompting like briefing a brilliant intern who knows nothing about your business. Give context, examples, constraints.

The rule: If your prompt could apply to anyone in any industry, your output will read like it was written for no one in particular.

The Formula
The context-rich prompt
formula that gets publishable output.
Five elements that transform any AI response.
The Context-Rich Prompt Template
[ROLE] You are a content strategist writing for [AUDIENCE] who struggles with [PROBLEM].
[TASK] Write a LinkedIn post that [SPECIFIC GOAL].
[CONSTRAINTS] Conversational tone, no buzzwords, under 300 words, end with a question.
[EXAMPLE] Here's an example of my voice: [paste 2–3 sentences of your writing].
Result: This prompt gets output you can publish. The generic prompt doesn't. The only difference is the specificity you bring to the brief.

Voice Training
Training AI on your voice —
the 30-minute setup.
One session. AI that sounds like you permanently.

Collect 10 of your best-performing posts. Paste them into Claude or GPT with:

'These are examples of my writing style. Analyse the voice, tone, vocabulary, and structure. From now on, write all content in this exact style.'

Save this context. Reference it in every content prompt. AI writing goes from 'obvious AI' to 'sounds exactly like me' in one session.

Pro tip: Create a saved prompt file with your voice description. Paste it at the start of every new AI conversation. You brief the AI once — it writes like you every time.

Beyond Writing
AI for research and ideation —
the prompts that save hours.
AI handles the 80% that drains your energy.
→
Content ideas: 'What are the 20 most common questions [your ideal client] asks about [your topic]? Rank them by urgency and relevance.'
→
Research summary: 'Summarise the key points from this article in bullet points: [paste article].'
→
SEO keywords: 'List 15 long-tail keyword variants of the phrase [keyword] that someone would search on Google.'
→
Competitor gap: 'Here are the top 10 posts in my niche this month. What topics are missing that my audience would want?'
The principle: Use AI to eliminate the research and ideation friction. Keep your expertise and opinion human. That's the division of labour that works.
