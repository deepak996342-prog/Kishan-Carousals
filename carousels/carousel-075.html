Google Business Profile
The 30-minute setup that
changes your local visibility.
Free. Takes 30 minutes. Ranks within 4–8 weeks.

Step 1: Claim your Google Business Profile (free at business.google.com).
Step 2: Add your business name, service area, keyword-rich description, services list, and 5+ photos.
Step 3: Get 5–10 client reviews.
That's it. For service businesses without a physical office, set your service area to your city or region. You'll appear in local searches within 4–8 weeks. This single setup is the highest-ROI 30 minutes in local SEO.

Local Keyword Strategy
The searches that bring
in-market, ready-to-hire clients.
Low competition, high intent, city-specific.

The local keyword formula is simple and powerful:
'[your service] + [city]': 'content strategy London' — direct, hire-intent search.
'best + [service] + near me': mobile-first, location-based search.
'[your name] + [service]': brand searches from people who've heard of you — optimise your personal brand SEO too.
One city-specific service page per service you offer is enough to start. These low-competition, high-intent searches are answered by local SEO — and most local competitors have nothing.

Google Reviews
The ethical review strategy
that builds local authority.
10+ reviews establishes trust and raises your local ranking.

After every successful engagement, send a personal email: 'If you found [specific result you delivered] valuable, a Google review would help other [client type] like you find me. Here's the direct link.'
Personal, specific, genuine. 10+ reviews with responses establishes trust and pushes your profile higher in local results. Reviews with keywords in them (when clients naturally describe your service) provide an additional ranking signal.
Respond to every review within 24 hours — this shows Google your profile is actively managed.

Location Pages
Creating location pages
that rank for multiple cities.
One dedicated page per city creates a local SEO network.

If you serve multiple cities, create a dedicated page for each: '[Service] for businesses in [City]'.
Include: city-specific language, any local case studies if you have them, embedded Google map, 300–500 words minimum of unique content per page. Link between location pages to create a local SEO network.
This creates a local presence for each market you serve — without needing a physical office in each city. Each location page independently ranks for '[service] + [city]' searches within that market.

Start with one: Create your primary city page first. Rank it. Then expand to secondary cities once the first is performing.
