Buying Signals
Identifying buying signals
in email behaviour.
The gap between signal and response is where sales are won or lost.

Subscriber opens 5 emails in a row: warming up.
Clicks every link about pricing or process: researching.
Replies to your email with a service question: ready to buy.

Your email platform tracks all of this. Set up email notifications for these behaviours. When you see them: reach out personally within 24 hours. The gap between signal and response is where sales are won or lost.

The Conversion Email
The conversion email that makes
the ask without feeling pushy.
Specific. Honest. Low pressure. No fake scarcity.

The best conversion emails tell a client story first. Then:

'If any of this sounds like your situation, I'm opening [X] spots for [month]. Here's what we'd do together: [3 bullet points]. Investment: [range]. To see if it's a fit: [booking link].'

The story creates recognition; the offer creates a path. No fake scarcity. No countdown timers.
