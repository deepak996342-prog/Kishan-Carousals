The Ideation Problem
Why creators run out of ideas
after 30 posts.
AI turns audience intelligence into an endless content calendar.

Most creators exhaust their personal experience within the first 30 posts. After that: topic anxiety. The fix isn't writing more from personal experience — it's mining your audience's questions, fears, and aspirations systematically.

AI makes this research fast and specific, turning audience intelligence into an endless content calendar. The ideas were always there. AI helps you find them faster.

The insight: Your audience's questions are more valuable than your personal experiences as content seeds — because they answer demand that already exists.

20-Minute Ideation
The 20-minute AI ideation
session.
3 weeks of content in 20 minutes.

Open Claude. Prompt: 'I create content for [audience] on [topic]. Generate 30 specific content ideas covering: their biggest fears, common mistakes they make, frequently asked questions, and transformations they want. Format as: [Hook idea] + [what the post covers in one sentence].'

Edit out weak ideas. You now have 3 weeks of content in 20 minutes. The AI does the volume work. You do the quality curation.

Pattern Mining
Mining your existing content
for patterns.
AI reveals the gap between what you've published and what works.

Paste your top 10 posts into Claude: 'Here are 10 of my best-performing posts. What themes are emerging? What topics are connecting most with my audience? What related topics haven't I covered yet that my audience would want?'

This analysis reveals the content gap between what you've published and what your audience is ready for. Your data, interpreted by AI, tells you exactly where to go next.

The pattern test: Most creators find that 2-3 themes explain 80% of their best-performing posts. Find the pattern. Scale it.

Competitor Research
Using AI to monitor what's
working in your industry.
Use competitors' data to inform your positioning, not copy their approach.

Collect your competitors' top 10 posts from the last month. Input to Claude: 'What topics are performing well? What gaps exist that my audience would want? What contrarian takes could differentiate my content?'

AI identifies patterns in seconds that would take hours of manual research. Use competitors' data to inform your positioning, not copy their approach. The goal is differentiation, not imitation.

Quarterly Strategy
The quarterly AI strategy session
that replaces 5 hours of planning.
AI sees patterns humans miss across all your variables simultaneously.

Every 3 months: 'I create content about [topics] for [audience]. Here are my top 10 posts from last quarter: [list]. Suggest 12 content themes for next quarter — each with 3 post ideas — balancing audience interest and business goals.'

This single session replaces 5 hours of planning and produces better results because AI sees patterns humans miss. Book a recurring calendar slot. 60 minutes. Every quarter.

The output: 12 themes × 3 post ideas = 36 posts planned before the quarter starts. That's 3 months of content in one focused session.
