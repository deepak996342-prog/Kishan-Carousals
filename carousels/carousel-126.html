What a Content Bank Is
Not a queue. A searchable
idea database.
The distinction most creators miss from day one.

A content bank is a searchable database of post ideas, topic angles, stories, frameworks, and hooks — not a queue of ready-to-publish posts.

The difference matters enormously. A queue creates a deadline treadmill. A bank creates a reservoir. When you sit down to write, you're not inventing — you're choosing. The creative friction drops to near zero and output becomes a function of time, not inspiration.

Think of it as your content intelligence layer.

Key distinction: A post queue runs out. A content bank grows. Every idea you add compounds — one idea branches into three when you return to it fresh.

5 Sources to Fill Fast
Where 200 ideas actually
come from.
You already have more than you think.

1. Things you explain to clients repeatedly. If you've said it twice, it's a post.
2. DM questions. Every question someone asks you in private is one their network wants answered publicly.
3. Myths you disagree with. Contrarian views drive more engagement than consensus.
4. Recent lessons. What went wrong this week? What worked unexpectedly? That's your content.
5. Your frameworks. Every process you use is a carousel waiting to be made.

Quick start: Open your sent messages right now. Count how many times you've explained something this week. That's your first 10 bank entries.

Bank Organisation System
Structure makes the bank
actually usable.
A bank you can't navigate is just a messy document.

The simplest Notion structure that works: a table with 5 columns — Topic | Pillar | Post Type | Status | Date Added.

Tag every entry by theme (Mindset / Strategy / Tactics / Story / Frameworks) so you can filter when you need a specific type. Status options: Raw Idea → Draft → Scheduled → Published. Never delete published entries — they're your future repurposing library.

Spend 30 minutes building this once. The system pays dividends every week after.

Pro tip: Add a "Seasonality" tag (Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4) for ideas tied to time of year. Filter January's posts in December when you're planning ahead.

Weekly Maintenance
The 20-minute ritual that
keeps the bank full.
Maintenance beats motivation every time.

Every Monday (10 minutes): Add 5 new ideas. They don't need to be fleshed out — a title and one sentence is enough. Add what's on your mind from the previous week.

Every Friday (10 minutes): Review the bank. Promote the 2-3 best raw ideas to "Draft" status. Add any quick notes that would help future-you when you sit down to write them.

This 20-minute weekly habit is the entire system. It's not glamorous. That's why it works — it's too simple to procrastinate.

From 0 to 200
The 6-week build that
makes you content-rich.
The compound curve of a growing content bank.

Week 1 — Brain dump (2 hours): 60 ideas. Set a timer. Write every topic, question, story, myth, framework that comes to mind. Zero filtering. Volume is the goal.

Weeks 2-3 — Categorise and expand: 120 ideas. Sort your 60 into pillars. For each, write 1 variation. One idea becomes two. Easy.

Weeks 4-6 — Weekly habit: 200 ideas. Add 5 every Monday, review every Friday. By week 6 you cross 200 and never look back.

Milestone: At 200 entries, you have 8+ months of content at 3×/week. "What should I post?" is no longer a question in your life.
