Days 1–30 · Foundation
Set up everything
before you scale.
The first 30 days are about clarity, not reach.

Most people skip the foundation phase and wonder why their content doesn't land. Your first 30 days should be spent on: defining your niche and audience, optimising your profile to convert visitors, and publishing your first 10 posts to test what resonates.
Don't worry about reach in month one. Build the structure that reach can flow through. A leaky bucket gets bigger, not fixed.

Month 1 checklist: Profile fully optimised · Niche defined · Content pillars set · First 10 posts published · 50+ strategic connections added

Days 31–60 · Momentum
Build consistency and
start engaging daily.
The second 30 days are about showing up reliably.

By day 31, you know what type of content your audience responds to. Now the job is volume and consistency. Post 4–5 times per week. Comment on 10 posts per day. Start 3–5 genuine DM conversations. Add 200 new strategic connections.
This is when the algorithm begins to recognise you. Your audience starts expecting your content. The flywheel begins to turn — slowly at first, then faster.

Days 61–90 · Acceleration
Double down on
what's working.
The final 30 days are about compounding what you've learned.

By day 60, you have data. You know which posts drove DMs. You know which topics resonate. You know what time your audience is active. In days 61–90, stop experimenting and start scaling. Create more of what's already working. Repurpose your best posts.
This is when inbound starts arriving — enquiries from people who've been reading your content for two months and are now ready to reach out.

Acceleration rule: Find your 3 best-performing posts. Write 5 variations of each. That's your next 15 posts — no new ideation needed.

The Key Habits
3 daily habits that make
the roadmap actually work.
Small consistent inputs create exponential outputs.

1. Write every morning. Even 15 minutes of writing before your day starts builds a content bank that sustains you through low-motivation weeks. Ideas compound like interest — you need the account.
2. Engage before you post. Spend 10 minutes commenting on others' posts before publishing your own. The algorithm boosts active users who engage, not just broadcast.
3. Reply within the first hour. Every comment on your post in the first 60 minutes signals value to the algorithm. Be there to reply.

The Trap
Don't let perfectionism
kill your momentum.
Done and published beats perfect and never posted.

The biggest threat to your first 90 days isn't the algorithm — it's your own standards getting in the way of consistency. Perfectionism masquerades as quality control, but it's usually fear. Fear of judgment. Fear of being wrong. Fear of not sounding smart enough.
Your 10th post will be better than your 1st. Your 50th will be better than your 10th. The only way to get to better is to publish the imperfect version right now.

The 80% rule: If a post is 80% ready, it's ready. Publish it. The feedback will teach you more than another hour of editing.
