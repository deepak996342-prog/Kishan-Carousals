Topical Depth Wins
Why Google rewards depth
over breadth — always.
The most important SEO insight for service businesses in 2025.

A website with 20 articles about LinkedIn strategy will outrank a website with 200 articles covering every social platform. Google's algorithm identifies 'topical authority' — sites that cover a topic comprehensively rank higher for all terms within that topic.
This is the single most important SEO insight for service businesses. You don't need to cover everything. You need to cover your thing better than anyone else online. Deep beats broad. Specialised beats general. This is the unfair advantage of being a focused specialist.

Pillar + Cluster Model
The architecture that signals
expertise to Google.
An interconnected web of expertise that Google can traverse.

Pillar page: comprehensive guide on a broad topic — 'The Complete Guide to LinkedIn Content Strategy' (3,000+ words covering everything).
Cluster articles: 10–15 deep-dives into subtopics from the pillar — 'LinkedIn Hook Formulas', 'LinkedIn Profile Optimisation', 'LinkedIn Content Calendar'.
Each cluster article links to the pillar. The pillar links to all cluster articles. Google sees an interconnected web of expertise. It recognises you as the authoritative source on this topic and ranks you accordingly.

Planning Your Cluster
How to plan a content cluster
for your service.
10–12 weeks from zero to topical authority.

Step 1: Choose 2–3 topics you want to own in Google search results.
Step 2: Write the pillar page first (3,000+ words, covers the topic broadly).
Step 3: List 10 subtopics that relate to the pillar — each becomes a cluster article.
Step 4: Write one cluster article per week.
In 10–12 weeks, you have a complete topical authority cluster. Then repeat for the next topic. Two clusters per year. That's 20–30 articles of deep, interconnected expertise that Google cannot ignore.

Momentum note: Publish the pillar first. Every cluster article you add after makes the pillar rank better. Progress is visible from week 4.

Linking Architecture
The linking structure that
makes clusters work.
The topic web Google can traverse — and rewards.

Each cluster article includes three things:
1. A link to the pillar in the introduction — establishes topical connection immediately.
2. Links to 2–3 other cluster articles — creates the interconnected web.
3. A CTA at the end linking to your service page — turns readers into leads.
The pillar becomes the most authoritative page on the topic on your site. This creates a 'topic web' that Google can traverse — and the more connections you add, the more the whole cluster benefits from every new article you publish.

Measuring Authority Growth
Signs your topical authority
is compounding.
Traffic that grows without additional effort — the cluster effect.

Within 3 months: individual cluster articles start ranking for their specific keywords.
Within 6 months: the pillar page starts ranking for the broad topic term.
Within 9 months: new articles you add to the cluster rank faster because the cluster's accumulated authority lifts them automatically.
This is the compounding effect. Traffic grows without proportional effort. The cluster you built in month 1 earns traffic in month 24. Each new article you add benefits from everything that came before it.

The milestone: When new articles rank within days of publishing — that's topical authority confirmed. You've built something Google trusts.
